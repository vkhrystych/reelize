FAQ

Short answers.

How long does one video take?

Roughly 3–5 minutes for a 2-hour podcast, end to end. Longer videos queue in the background and you get an email when the pack is ready.

Do I have to own the video?

You need the rights to the content you upload or link. Reelize is built for creators cutting their own long-form — we're not a rip tool for someone else's channel.

Can I edit the clips afterwards?

Yes. Trim the in/out points, swap caption styles, re-crop, and re-export — or download the SRT and finish in Premiere or CapCut.

What languages work?

40+ for transcription, including Ukrainian, Polish, German, Spanish and Portuguese. Highlight detection is strongest in English and Ukrainian today; the rest catch up each month.

What does early access get me?

Free credits at launch, founder pricing locked for 12 months, and a direct line to us while we tune the scoring model.
